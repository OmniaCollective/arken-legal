Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 March 2026  ·  Last updated: 1 March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arken Invest ("Arken", "we", "us") handles your information when you use our mobile application. We take your privacy seriously and have designed the app to collect as little data as possible.

1. Information You Provide

When you use Arken Invest, you may enter the following information directly into the app:

  • Your first name (used for personalised greetings)
  • Investment holdings — fund names, values, and charge rates (OCF)
  • Currency preference (GBP or USD)

This data is stored locally on your device only. It is not uploaded to our servers, not shared with third parties, and not linked to any account.

2. Voice Input

The "Ask Arken" feature allows you to speak questions about your portfolio. When you use this feature, your voice recording is sent to a third-party AI processing service to generate a response. Voice data is not stored by Arken after processing and is not linked to your identity.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

Arken Invest does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials
  • Financial account numbers or institution data
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers for tracking or advertising
  • Usage analytics or crash reports (beyond what Apple provides to all developers)

4. Third-Party Services

Voice queries are processed by an AI service provider. This service receives only the audio of your question and the text of your portfolio holdings as context. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or social login providers.

5. Children's Privacy

Arken Invest is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
